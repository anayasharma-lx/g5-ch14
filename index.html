Compass Directions
A compass has 4 main directions: North, South, East, West — and 4 in-between: NE, SE, SW, NW. North always points to the top of the map. Use the compass and map knowledge to answer each question below.
Compass Rose
Turn clockwise:
N → E → S → W → N

NE is between North and East, NW between North and West, etc.

Map Scale
A scale converts map distances to real distances. On this map, 1 cm = 2 km. To find real distance: map cm × scale = real km. To find map distance: real km ÷ scale = map cm.
